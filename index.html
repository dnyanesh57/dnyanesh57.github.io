About

Engineer first.
Then the code.

I work where structures, quality and software meet, and I measure my work by what changes on site.

At SJ Contracts Pvt. Ltd., an established Pune building contractor, I am Structural Design Coordinator and Assistant Manager, Quality & Digitalization. I coordinate structural, architectural and MEPF design and manage clashes before drawings reach site, review structural designs for design-build bids, and lead quality digitalization across 25+ projects: 10+ processes digitised, 10+ real-time dashboards and a 90% cut in reporting time.

Before that I spent three years at C-Probe Technologies, a concrete-monitoring start-up. I joined as a research engineer and went on to lead the company: 50+ site deployments, investor pitches that secured ₹1 Crore in funding, ₹50 Lakh+ in sales, and a Best Startup Award. My M.Tech research on predicting concrete properties with the maturity method has been adopted by Tata Projects, SJ Contracts and UltraTech Cement.

My BIM practice covers the full chain: Revit modelling across disciplines, Navisworks coordination and clash detection, and 4D/5D simulation in Bexel Manager and Navisworks Timeliner. I'm building toward computational structural engineering: structural BIM automation, ETABS and SAP2000 through Python, and structural data science.

Method

How I work

The same four steps whether the problem is a crane, a column or a quality review.

STEP 01

Measure

Get real data from site: observations, test results, survey points, audit findings.

→ crane logs, DigiQC, survey CSVs
STEP 02

Model

Turn it into a structure: an analysis model, a BIM model or a clean dataset.

→ STAAD.Pro, ETABS, Revit, Navisworks, Bexel
STEP 03

Automate

Script the repetitive parts so they're fast, consistent and checkable.

→ pyRevit, Dynamo, Python
STEP 04

Report

Hand decision-makers a clear story they can act on.

→ dashboards, clash reports, MSP schedules
